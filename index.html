Hunt Hypothesis Builder

Rule-based, not AI: each recent article is matched on what it contains (CVE IDs, defanged IOCs, ransomware, phishing, supply chain, APT, malware) and turned into a hypothesis with a Defender XDR advanced hunting query built from the article's own details. Uses the articles currently shown in the Threat Intel tab.

OSINT Tools

Paste an indicator (IP, domain, URL, hash or CVE - defanged is fine), then click a tile or press its letter. Tiles marked lookup open the result directly; the others open the tool's home page and copy the indicator to your clipboard. Letter shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a field.

IOC Extractor

Extracts IPv4/IPv6, domains, URLs, email addresses, MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256/SHA-512 hashes and CVE IDs. Defanged input is handled: hxxp, [.], (.), [dot], [:], [at]. Everything runs locally in your browser.
